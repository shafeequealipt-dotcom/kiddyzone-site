## Toys and Games for Little Learners

Toys that promote sensory play, like playdough, stacking cups, or shape sorters, are perfect for one-year-olds in Kerala, as they help develop fine motor skills and hand-eye coordination. These types of toys are also great for hot summer days when outdoor play may not be possible.

Music instruments, like xylophones or drums, are another excellent option, as they introduce children to the world of music and encourage creativity, which is deeply rooted in Kerala's culture.

For a more traditional gift, consider a beautifully crafted wooden toy, like a puzzle or a push-pull toy, which is both educational and fun, and can be enjoyed during family gatherings and celebrations.

- Sensory play toys like playdough and stacking cups
- Music instruments like xylophones and drums
- Wooden toys like puzzles and push-pull toys
