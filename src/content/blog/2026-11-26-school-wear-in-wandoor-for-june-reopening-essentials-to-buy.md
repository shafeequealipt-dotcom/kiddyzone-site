## What Can Wait

While it's tempting to buy everything at once, there are some items that can wait until later. For example, you may not need to buy a new set of clothes for every single day of the week, especially if your child already has a few sets that are still in good condition. Consider waiting until the monsoon season is over before buying any new clothes that may be prone to water damage.

Another item that can wait is a new set of accessories, such as hats, scarves, or jewelry. While these can be fun and add a personal touch to your child's outfit, they are not essential and can be purchased at a later time. Focus on buying the essentials first, and then you can consider adding accessories later on.

It's also important to consider your child's personal style and preferences when making purchases. If they have a favorite color or style, try to incorporate that into your buying decisions. This will help ensure that they feel confident and happy in what they're wearing.

- New sets of clothes for every day
- Accessories like hats and jewelry

## Monsoon Fashion Tips

The monsoon season in Kerala can be challenging when it comes to fashion, but there are a few tips and tricks you can follow to keep your child's clothes looking their best. One of the most important things is to choose clothes that are breathable and quick-drying, as these will help keep your child cool and dry even in the rain.

Another tip is to opt for clothes with a water-repellent coating, as these can help protect against water damage. You can also consider buying clothes with a bit of stretch, as these will be more comfortable and flexible in the rain.

Finally, don't forget to wash and dry your child's clothes regularly, especially during the monsoon season. This will help prevent the growth of mold and mildew, and keep their clothes looking their best.

- Breathable and quick-drying clothes
- Water-repellent coating
- Clothes with stretch
